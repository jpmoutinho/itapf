# Store listing copy

## Name

ITAPF — r/itookapicture Filter

## Short description

Hide NSFW and portrait posts from r/itookapicture while browsing Reddit.

## Description

ITAPF keeps r/itookapicture feeds focused on the photos you want to see. Use the toolbar popup to hide NSFW posts, portrait posts, or both.

It works across feeds on current and old Reddit. Choices update open tabs and are remembered locally. Direct post pages stay accessible.

ITAPF contains no ads, analytics, tracking, remote code, or account system.

## Permission explanations

**Storage:** Saves the user's Hide NSFW and Hide portraits choices in local extension storage.

**Reddit site access:** Reads visible post cards on Reddit and hides matching posts from r/itookapicture. No browsing or post data is transmitted.
