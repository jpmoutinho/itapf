# ITAPF Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

ITAPF does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising, remote code, or a backend service.

The extension reads post information already displayed on Reddit solely to determine whether a visible r/itookapicture post matches an enabled filter. This processing happens locally in the browser. The two filter preferences are saved in the browser's local extension storage and are not transmitted by ITAPF.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the support contact listed on the extension's store page.
